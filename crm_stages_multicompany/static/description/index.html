Multi-Company-Specific CRM Stages Ahex Technologies

This plugin enhances the CRM module by introducing company-specific stages. In the standard Odoo CRM, stages created are global and shared across all companies, which often limits flexibility in multi-company environments. With this customization, each company can define and manage its own pipeline stages independently, ensuring that workflows are tailored to their unique business processes. This provides greater control, avoids confusion caused by shared stages across companies, and helps maintain cleaner data separation within a multi-company setup.

STEP-BY-STEP WORKFLOW

Step 1: Create Stages from kanban view

Before Creating the stages select company in which you need to modify the stages,once you add the stages it will automatically maps to the current company and reflects in the List view also

Step 2: All stages for all companies from menu through developer mode

Flow the below steps to get the stages from the menu:
- Go to configuration-->pipeline -->stages
- You can group by company and check the stages company wise
